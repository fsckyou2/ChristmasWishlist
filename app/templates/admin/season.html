Do this after Christmas, once last year's gifts have been given.

What will happen

  • 🧾 {{ preview.claims }} claim(s) are cleared, so everyone's "My Gifts for Others" starts empty.
  • 📜 Archived gifts appear on each recipient's Gifts You've Received page, including who they were from.
  • 📋 {{ preview.remaining_after }} of {{ preview.total_items }} item(s) remain on lists afterwards. No digest email is sent about the removals.

This can't be undone from the app, and it reveals who gave what. Don't run it before the gifts have been opened.
