{# Step 1: Welcome #}

Welcome to Christmas Wishlist!

Let's take a quick tour to show you how to make your holiday gift-giving easier and more organized.

This will only take a minute, or you can skip it anytime.

{# Step 2: Your Wishlist #}
📝

Create Your Wishlist

Start by adding items to your own wishlist. You can add:

  • ✓ Products from any online store (we'll auto-fill details!)
  • ✓ Your own items with your own descriptions
  • ✓ Multiple quantities if you want more than one

Click "My Wishlist" in the navigation bar to get started.

{# Step 3: Browse Others #}
👀

Browse Other Wishlists

See what your family members want! Click "Browse Wishlists" to:

  • ✓ View everyone's wishlists
  • ✓ Pick out what you'll give
  • ✓ See what others have claimed, so nobody gets it twice

🔒 The person whose list it is can't see what's claimed or by whom.

{# Step 4: Claim Gifts #}
🎯

Say "I'll get this"

When you find something you want to give:

  • ✓ Click "I'll get this" to claim it
  • ✓ Everyone else sees it's claimed, so nobody gets it twice
  • ✓ It's hidden from the person it's for, so the surprise is safe
  • ✓ Changed your mind? Click "Undo"

Everything you're getting shows up in "Gifts I'm Giving".

{# Step 5: Track Progress #}
✅

Track Your Gift Progress

In "Gifts I'm Giving", tick off each step:

  • 💳 Bought - when you buy it
  • 📦 Arrived - when it's in your hands
  • 🎁 Wrapped - when it's ready to give

Stay organized throughout the holiday season!

{# Step 6: Custom Gifts #}
✨

Surprise Gifts

Want to give something that's not on their list?

Click "Add a surprise gift" on their wishlist to add it yourself. It's hidden from them, but everyone else sees it, marked as yours, so nobody gets it twice.

Perfect for those special surprises! 🎉

{# Step 7: Ready! #}
🎉

You're All Set!

You're ready to make this holiday season stress-free and organized.

Happy gift-giving! 🎁

Click "Get Started" to begin using Christmas Wishlist!
